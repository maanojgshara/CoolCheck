Append one dated entry to the end of `docs/build-log.md` for the work done in this session. $ARGUMENTS

Use exactly these headings, in this order, with short bullet points:

- **Tried and approach:** what was attempted and how, including approaches that were dropped.
- **Learned:** facts found, with the number or source when there is one.
- **Decisions and why:** each decision with its reason, and the alternative that was rejected.
- **Stuck:** errors and blockers, how each was resolved or that it is still open. Quote exact error text.
- **Challenges and questions:** anything unresolved, and questions for the mentor.

Rules:
- Facts only. Only write results that were actually produced in this session; no placeholders and no guesses presented as facts.
- Never include tokens, passwords, keys, personal data or full workspace URLs.
- Keep it under 25 lines. Do not edit earlier entries.
- Do not commit; leave that to me.
